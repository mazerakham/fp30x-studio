The formulation

Normalise both performances to [0,1], so the identity exists in the space. Write φ=id+u with u(0)=u(1)=0. Then φ′=1+u′, the homeomorphism condition is exactly u′>−1, and we minimise

E[u]= ∑i (ti+u (ti)− si)2 +λ∫01 |u′|2

over H01. Because the endpoint constraint fixes ∫01φ′=1, completing the square gives ∫01|φ′|2=1+∥φ′−1∥L22 — so the penalty is exactly the variance of tempo about its own mean. Minimising it is minimising rubato in the plainest possible sense, and the identity is its unique zero.

Euler–Lagrange gives u″=0 away from the knots, so the minimiser is piecewise linear with corners at matched onsets. The corners are kept deliberately: a pianist changes tempo abruptly at phrase boundaries, and an H² penalty would erase exactly the feature worth seeing.

The Fisher–Rao angle is the better cross-piece statistic. The Ḣ1 metric on Diff⁺([0,1]) is Fisher–Rao up to a factor of ¼, with the square-root velocity map ψ=φ′ as the isometry, so distance is arc length on a sphere: d=arccos⟨φ1′,φ2′⟩. It is dimensionless, bounded by 90°, and comparable across pieces of different lengths — which a residual in seconds is not.

What to distrust

6 onsets in the false start found no partner, and 15 appeared only in the full performance. Of 73 onset clusters, 47 matched. A third of the false start is unaccounted for — ornaments taken differently, or notes simply not played the same. The warp describes the matched skeleton, not the whole performance.

λ landed at 0.631 by generalised cross-validation, giving 18.0 effective degrees of freedom over 47 knots — the curve is smoothed to about a third of the freedom the data would allow.

Timestamps sit on a 5.000 ms lattice imposed by the Bluetooth MIDI link. No claim here is finer than that. The constraint u′>−1 never went active, so φ is a genuine homeomorphism rather than one clipped into being.
